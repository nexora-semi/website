Precision timing chips, designed for performance and cost.

Nexora Semi is developing a family of timing ICs (TCXO, OCXO, PLL-based TCXO, clock generator and jitter cleaner ICs) on a reusable mixed-signal platform. Deep oscillator expertise, combined with an AI-assisted design flow, shortens development cycles and lowers cost.

The problem

Timing trade-offs that product designers live with

Precision timing parts used in telecommunications, optical networking, industrial electronics and connected devices must deliver high frequency stability, low phase noise, low power and competitive cost, all at once. Existing solutions involve trade-offs between these, especially at low supply voltages and across wide temperature ranges.

Product designers also have limited choice of timing devices optimised for their own power, performance and cost targets, and each new timing product traditionally takes months of specialist precision analog design.

Our solution

Integrated timing ICs, developed faster

Nexora is developing highly integrated mixed-signal timing ICs aimed at better performance-to-cost and power-to-performance ratios. A reusable platform (oscillator, temperature sensing, digital compensation, non-volatile calibration and power management) is designed to support many products and supply-voltage variants.

To develop it efficiently, Nexora has built a design flow in which AI agents do the time-consuming engineering work: schematic entry, simulation set-up and corner sweeps, layout placement and routing, DRC/LVS fixing and report writing. They drive the design tools through scripts, performing the same steps a conventional team would do by hand.

An experienced oscillator and timing-IC designer sets the architecture, reviews every stage and signs off.

Early results

Measured on real circuit blocks

Block-level results from Nexora's internal development, October 2026.

~15 blocks

Analog blocks (regulators, bias generators, LDOs, oscillator, divider) laid out by AI agents and verified DRC/LVS-clean in about two weeks, reviewed by one designer.

< 1 minute

For an automated placement, power, routing, DRC and parasitic-extraction pass on a block, so layout can be regenerated freely as the design evolves.

17% smaller

AI-generated regulator layout vs. the hand-drawn version, with about 6 dB better supply rejection. It replaced the hand layout as the reference design.

100+ corners

Process, voltage and temperature corners plus Monte Carlo runs, set up, run and checked automatically for each block.

A complete chip has not yet been taped out with this flow; full-chip integration is the current milestone. See R&D progress.
