AI-accelerated analog & mixed-signal IC design

Custom chips, designed in weeks, not months.

Nexora Semi pairs senior mixed-signal design expertise with an AI agent-driven design flow. AI agents generate, simulate, lay out and verify circuits inside industry-standard EDA tools, under the direction of one experienced designer, doing work that traditionally needs a team.

The problem

Custom silicon is too slow and too expensive for most products

A custom analog or mixed-signal chip can make a product smaller, lower-power and cheaper at volume. But designing even a small one typically takes around six months and a specialist team: an analog designer, a digital designer and a layout engineer.

Experienced mixed-signal engineers are scarce, and that cost and schedule put custom silicon out of reach for many small and mid-sized product companies. They settle for off-the-shelf parts instead.

Our solution

One senior designer, a team of AI agents

Nexora has built a design flow in which AI agents do the time-consuming engineering work: schematic entry, simulation set-up and corner sweeps, layout placement and routing, DRC/LVS fixing and report writing. They work directly in the same professional tools a conventional team would use.

An experienced mixed-signal designer sets the architecture, reviews every stage and signs off. The result: the quality of an expert team, at the speed and cost of one person.

Early results

Measured on real circuit blocks

Block-level results from Nexora's internal development, October 2026.

~15 blocks

Analog blocks (regulators, bias generators, LDOs, oscillator, divider) laid out by AI agents and verified DRC/LVS-clean in about two weeks, reviewed by one designer.

< 1 minute

For an automated placement, power, routing, DRC and parasitic-extraction pass on a block, so layout can be regenerated freely as the design evolves.

17% smaller

AI-generated regulator layout vs. the hand-drawn version, with about 6 dB better supply rejection. It replaced the hand layout as the reference design.

100+ corners

Process, voltage and temperature corners plus Monte Carlo runs, set up, run and checked automatically for each block.

A complete chip has not yet been taped out with this flow; full-chip integration is the current milestone. See R&D progress.
