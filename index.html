Why Nexora exists

Faster, leaner semiconductor development

Integrated-circuit development is constrained by long design cycles, high engineering cost and limited iteration speed. In precision timing, every product must balance frequency stability, phase noise, power and cost across temperature and supply, and each new product or variant traditionally takes months of specialist work.

Nexora applies AI-assisted methods across simulation, verification, layout automation and engineering analysis, inside a rigorous silicon design methodology, so we can develop more products and variants with a lean team.

Current development

Our first mixed-signal platform

Nexora is developing its first mixed-signal semiconductor platform for precision timing and frequency-control applications. It integrates analog circuitry, sensing, calibration, digital compensation and control into a highly integrated IC architecture designed to support multiple future products and variants.

First silicon is targeted for 2027.
