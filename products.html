Custom ASICs, and our own chips

Two ways the AI design flow creates value: designing custom mixed-signal chips for product companies, and developing Nexora's own standard products.

Custom ASIC design

Your chip, designed in weeks

For product companies that need lower power, a smaller size or a lower unit cost than off-the-shelf parts can deliver. We take a specification through circuit design, layout, verification and tape-out preparation using our AI agent-driven flow.

A smaller team and a shorter schedule mean a lower design cost, which makes custom silicon viable at volumes where it would not have been before.
