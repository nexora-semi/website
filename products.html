Products

Precision timing ICs

Every electronic system needs an accurate clock. Nexora is developing a family of timing ICs, from temperature-compensated and oven-controlled crystal oscillators to PLL-based programmable oscillators, clock generators and jitter cleaners. All are designed with our AI-accelerated design flow, so new frequencies and variants can reach the market quickly.

Product roadmap

Timing product families

TCXO ICs and PLL-based TCXOs are our first focus. All products are in development; contact us to discuss requirements or early access.

First focus

TCXO ICs

Temperature-compensated crystal oscillator ICs. Paired with a quartz crystal, on-chip temperature sensing and compensation, with digital calibration and trim memory, hold the output frequency stable from cold to hot.

  • GNSS and positioning
  • Wireless and IoT modules
  • Telecom and industrial equipment
First focus

PLL-based TCXOs

A temperature-compensated crystal reference combined with an integrated fractional-N PLL that synthesises the required output frequency. One crystal covers many frequencies, so customers get programmable frequencies and short lead times.

  • Flexible-frequency reference clocks
  • Communications and networking
  • Consolidating multiple oscillators
Roadmap

OCXO controller ICs

Oscillator and precision oven-control circuits for oven-controlled crystal oscillators: the highest-stability class of crystal oscillator, holding the crystal at a constant temperature.

  • Telecom infrastructure and network synchronisation
  • Test and measurement
  • Precision time and frequency references
Roadmap

Clock generators

Multi-output, programmable clock generators with low jitter, generating the many clock frequencies a board needs from a single reference.

  • Processors, FPGAs and SoCs
  • Networking and storage
  • Data-converter systems
Roadmap

Jitter cleaners

Jitter attenuators that take a noisy reference or recovered clock and output a clean, low-jitter clock locked to it.

  • High-speed serial links
  • 5G and wireless infrastructure
  • Data-centre equipment

Why AI matters for timing

More variants, faster, from a small team

Timing products sell in many variants: different frequencies, stability grades, supply voltages, output types and packages. Each new variant or product generation traditionally needs months of precision analog design, layout and verification across temperature.

Nexora's AI agent-driven flow regenerates, re-simulates and re-lays-out circuit blocks automatically under expert review. That lets one experienced timing-IC designer build and maintain a broad portfolio and respond quickly to customer needs.

What makes a timing IC hard

  • Frequency stability over temperature, supply and ageing
  • Ultra-low phase noise and jitter
  • Low power and fast, reliable start-up
  • Precise, per-part calibration stored on chip
  • Verification across many process and temperature corners

These are exactly the repetitive, simulation-heavy tasks our AI agents automate.
