About us

A fabless team focused on precision

Nexora Semiconductor Pty Ltd (Nexora Semi) is a new fabless semiconductor company founded by analog and mixed-signal IC designers.

Our approach

Every Nexora part combines a carefully designed analog core with digital calibration. Each part is trimmed individually and the result is stored in on-chip non-volatile memory, so it meets spec without external components.

We work on mature, high-volume processes, which keep our products cost-effective and our supply chain dependable.

Design

Full custom analog, RF and digital design in-house.

Verification

Corner, Monte Carlo and system-level verification before tape-out.

Characterization

Bench validation over temperature, supply and process.
