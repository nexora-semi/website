About us

Precision timing, from a new kind of chip company

Nexora Semiconductor Pty Ltd (Nexora Semi) is a fabless semiconductor startup developing precision timing ICs. We combine deep oscillator and mixed-signal design experience with AI-assisted engineering to develop products faster and at lower cost.

Our mission

Every connected, communicating or measuring device depends on an accurate clock. Nexora's mission is to deliver timing ICs with better performance-to-cost and power-to-performance ratios, built on a reusable mixed-signal platform that can serve many products and variants.

We develop our IP in South Australia, manufacture through established international foundry and packaging partners, and sell worldwide, building Australian capability in precision semiconductor design.

Founder & Chief Designer

Oscillator and mixed-signal ASIC designer

Professional experience developing oscillator and PLL products and multiple mixed-signal ASIC tape-outs, from specification through to silicon. Architect of Nexora's AI-assisted design flow.

Vision

High-performance silicon, faster to develop and more affordable to deploy

Nexora aims to build a scalable portfolio of semiconductor products by combining specialist IC engineering, reusable silicon IP and AI-enabled development workflows. Over time, this approach enables more product variants, shorter design cycles and lower semiconductor development cost.

South Australia

Designed in South Australia. Built for global markets.

Nexora Semiconductor is headquartered in Adelaide, South Australia. Core semiconductor R&D, architecture development and intellectual property are developed in South Australia, with international partners supporting fabrication, packaging and production.

We aim to contribute to South Australia's growing capability in advanced electronics, semiconductor design and high-value technology exports.

Frequently asked questions

What does Nexora Semi do?

We are developing precision timing ICs: oscillator ICs and PLL oscillator ICs first, then MEMS oscillator ICs, clock generators and jitter cleaners, on a reusable mixed-signal platform designed with an AI-assisted flow.

How does Nexora use AI in chip design?

AI agents operate chip-design tools by script across schematic, simulation, layout and verification. A senior designer reviews every stage.

Does AI replace the chip designer?

No. The designer sets the architecture and specifications and signs off each stage. The agents take on time-consuming execution work.

What has the flow achieved so far?

About 15 analog blocks have been laid out by AI agents and verified DRC/LVS-clean in roughly two weeks.

What is the competitive advantage?

The flow aims to shorten mixed-signal IC development substantially with a lean team. These are targets based on block-level results; full-chip validation is our current milestone.

What happens next?

Full-chip integration, tape-out of our first precision-timing ASIC, silicon characterisation and engineering samples. See R&D progress.
