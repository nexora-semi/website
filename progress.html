R&D progress

From a working flow to first silicon

Where Nexora is today, and the milestones ahead. We report results as measured, and mark plans clearly as plans.

Milestones

  1. 2026 · Completed

    AI design flow built

    Automated schematic, simulation, layout and verification, driven by AI agents.

  2. October 2026 · Completed

    Proven on real circuit blocks

    About 15 analog blocks laid out by AI agents and verified DRC/LVS-clean in roughly two weeks.

  3. 2026 · Completed

    Nexora Semiconductor Pty Ltd established

    Company formed (ABN registered October 2026) to develop and sell precision timing ICs.

  4. Now · In progress

    Full-chip integration

    Extending the flow from blocks to a complete chip.

  5. Planned

    First timing ASIC tape-out

    Nexora's first precision-timing mixed-signal ASIC, the basis of a reusable oscillator platform, fabricated on a multi-project wafer (MPW) shuttle.

  6. Planned

    Silicon characterisation

    Measuring the first silicon against simulation.

  7. Planned

    Engineering samples and team growth

    Engineering samples for customer evaluation and design-in, further timing products from the same platform, and a growing South Australian engineering team.

What has been demonstrated

Demonstrated

  • AI agents generating schematics and layouts by driving design tools through scripts
  • Automated DRC/LVS-clean layouts on about 15 analog blocks
  • Automated corner and Monte Carlo verification

Not yet demonstrated

  • A complete chip designed end to end with the flow
  • Fabricated silicon from the AI flow

These are the objectives of our next milestones.
