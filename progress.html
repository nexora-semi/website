R&D progress

From a working flow to first silicon

Where Nexora is today, and the milestones ahead. We report results as measured, and mark plans clearly as plans.

Milestones

  1. 2026 · Completed

    AI design flow built

    Headless schematic generation, automated simulation harnesses (corners, Monte Carlo, noise), scripted layout and automated DRC/LVS/extraction, coordinated across multiple AI agents.

  2. October 2026 · Completed

    Proven on real circuit blocks

    About 15 analog blocks laid out by AI agents and verified DRC/LVS-clean in roughly two weeks, with one designer reviewing. One AI-generated layout outperformed the hand layout and replaced it as the reference.

  3. 2026 · Completed

    Nexora Semiconductor Pty Ltd established

    Company formed to commercialise the flow through custom ASIC design and its own products.

  4. Now · In progress

    Full-chip integration

    Extending the flow from blocks to a complete chip: top-level assembly, pad ring and chip-level verification. This is the step that will demonstrate the ~1-month design target end to end.

  5. Planned

    First tape-out

    Fabrication of a first AI-flow-designed chip on a cost-sharing multi-project wafer (MPW) shuttle.

  6. Planned

    Silicon validation

    Bench measurement of the fabricated chip against simulation, to confirm the flow produces working silicon first time.

  7. Planned

    First customer projects and team growth

    Custom ASIC engagements with product companies, and building a South Australian design team around the flow.

What has been demonstrated

Demonstrated

  • AI agents generating schematics and layouts in industry-standard tools
  • Automated DRC/LVS-clean layouts on about 15 analog blocks
  • Under-a-minute automated layout and verification passes per block
  • An AI layout beating a hand layout on area and supply rejection
  • Automated multi-corner and Monte Carlo verification with reports

Not yet demonstrated

  • A complete chip designed end to end in ~1 month
  • Fabricated silicon from the AI flow
  • Digital synthesis and place-and-route inside the same agent flow (small control logic is already handled)

These are the objectives of our next milestones.
